NovaTech FAQ

Q: How can I track my order?
A: Use the tracking link emailed after dispatch.

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days; express shipping takes 1–2.

Q: Can I cancel an order?
A: Yes, within two hours of placing it if fulfillment has not begun.

Q: Which payment methods are supported?
A: Visa, Mastercard, NovaPay, and PayPal.

Q: Can I change my delivery address?
A: Contact support before the order is dispatched.

Q: How do I contact support?
A: Email support@novatech.example, Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 fictional local time.
